= Product Capability Portfolio Audit
:revdate: 2026-09-20

== Scope

This portfolio audit covers customer/business-facing platform components:

Cataloging, Retailing, Carting, Ordering, Pricing, Promoting, Paying, Billing, Taxating, Stocking, Shipping, Vendoring, Commissioning, Subscripting, Rewarding, Walleting, Exchanging, Currencing, Tagging, Faceting, Indexing, Searching, Locating, Addressing, Relating, and Messaging.

Technical enablers such as Objecting, Cruding, Collectioning, Tabling, Viewing, Interfacing, Administering, Bridging, Gating, Canonization, and Documentating are intentionally excluded from commerce-feature ranking. Their concern is architecture/platform support rather than merchant/customer business capability.

== Benchmark basis

The external baseline is the current public capability surface of Medusa v2, Vendure, and Sylius as of 2026-09-20. The comparison uses AHEAD, PARITY, PARTIAL, MISSING, and NOT_APPLICABLE conservatively. AHEAD is used only where the local repository exposes a materially stronger modeled or operational capability than the ordinary commerce-core baseline.

== Highest-priority business gaps

=== Tier 0: missing commerce engines

. Pricing: deterministic price-set/list/rule/tier selection with history/explanation.
. Stocking: per-location levels, ATP, reservations, movement ledger, idempotency and concurrency.
. Promoting: condition/action engine, coupons, stacking, campaign budget and redemption ledger.
. Rewarding: immutable points ledger, earn/redeem/expire/reverse, tiers and benefits.

These four are foundational. They should be built before adding adjacent new repositories.

=== Tier 1: cross-component transaction flows

. Cart -> Pricing/Promoting/Taxating/Stocking -> Ordering deterministic checkout.
. Ordering cancellation/return/exchange -> Stocking + Shipping + Paying + Billing reconciliation.
. Paying + Walleting split tender and refund-to-wallet.
. Shipping reverse logistics and multi-shipment/multi-location flows.
. Subscription plan change/proration/dunning across Subscripting + Pricing + Billing + Paying.

=== Tier 2: catalog/discovery completeness

. Finish Cataloging Product/Variant/Option/Attribute vocabulary and retire Category compatibility.
. Complete Faceting range/multi-select/aggregation semantics.
. Complete Searching synonyms, typo tolerance, boosting/pinning and zero-result behavior.
. Stabilize Retailing channel/store assortment and scheduled publication.

=== Tier 3: marketplace and retention

. Vendoring onboarding/KYC and seller-order/listing references.
. Commissioning versioned rules, reversals, disputes and settlement reconciliation.
. Rewarding tiers/benefits after ledger correctness.
. Messaging moderation/retention and commerce-aware notification/thread references where justified.

== Coverage gaps that require ownership verification before creating anything

The competitor baselines expose several first-class domains that are not named as standalone repositories in this audited business portfolio. This is not an instruction to create repositories. First locate existing owners and prove a real gap:

* customer/account identity and customer groups;
* authentication/user/role/access policy;
* store/sales-channel configuration;
* region/market configuration;
* translation/localization master policy;
* gift-card lifecycle;
* returns/exchanges orchestration;
* fulfillment abstraction if Shipping does not intentionally own the full fulfillment boundary;
* product reviews/ratings if they are in product scope.

Some of these may already belong to components outside this business-audit set. Ownership must be resolved against live Canonization before materialization.

== Boundary watch list

Cataloging::
Legacy Category terminology and compatibility APIs can obscure the actual Product/Catalog boundary.

Retailing::
Legacy tenantId transport compatibility must converge to canonical owner/vendor identity; Retailing must not become a tenancy subsystem.

Addressing::
Objecting owns reusable system fields. Duplicate state/audit mappings must be removed rather than normalized as Addressing business data.

Subscripting::
Legacy Couponing/Discounting wording must converge to Promoting.

Carting/Ordering::
Keep the commitment boundary explicit: mutable intent in Carting; committed order facts in Ordering.

Pricing/Promoting/Taxating/Stocking::
These are independent engines. Carting/Ordering consume their immutable results/snapshots instead of reimplementing their logic.

Rewarding/Walleting::
Points and status are non-monetary Rewarding concerns. Store credit and money-like balances belong to Walleting.

Locating/Addressing::
Locating computes and orchestrates providers; Addressing persists durable address truth/evidence.

Indexing/Searching/Faceting::
Indexing owns index lifecycle; Searching owns query/ranking/tuning; Faceting owns filter semantics.

== Recommended milestone sequence

. Pricing M1-M2.
. Stocking M1-M2.
. Promoting M1-M2.
. Checkout integration slice: Carting + the three engines + Taxating -> Ordering.
. Rewarding M1-M2 and Walleting store-credit profile.
. Ordering returns/exchanges + Shipping reverse logistics + Paying/Billing reconciliation.
. Cataloging/Retailing/Faceting/Searching completeness.
. Subscripting proration/dunning.
. Marketplace depth: Vendoring + Commissioning.
. Secondary experience capabilities: Messaging, Relating and discovery refinements.

== Audit-file contract

Each audited business repository owns a root PRODUCT_CAPABILITY_AUDIT.adoc. Future implementation waves should update that file when a capability moves between MISSING, PARTIAL, PARITY or AHEAD, and must record any ownership transfer rather than silently duplicating behavior.
